She was simplicity and credulity.

Naive was her smile,

Shiny eyes and lush black hair

Like stars in the night sky.

She was special, out of her constellation.

Excogitative she was in life, hypnotic.

But a round eyed innocent,

Driven into a radius, with spittles around.

Oblivious in mind and body, because

She spiraled deep into her own oneness

For her trust, in the world was immense.

The bell then clinged, like in the gallows

A ruffian to enter

Camouflaged like a MAN

The daemon's eye glaced a covering of red

Intense, cold and hard just like his hold

But then hers, blacked out.

Incognizant of the evil around,

She cringed and then kayoed,

There she lay filling her chalice in blood.

Then there was silence

That deafened her in numbness.

Pain, was no sensation to her

Hardbound she stayed, immovable

She was not shocked

For she now was immortal.
