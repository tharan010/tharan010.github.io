In the soot of darkness I seek my soul

To bring me back to life, I pray!

Lost in deep waters, I find tranquillity

The fullness of home I find,

In the tides!

Like that of a flowing river,

My thoughts flow, cleansing me;

With every breadth, I see magic!

And I now believe,

There's a magic in me,

There's a magic in you!

All you need is a willing eye.

The sparkle of goodness

And contemplation of the same

Will help make better.

Hope for hope!
