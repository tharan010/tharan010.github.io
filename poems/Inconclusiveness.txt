You walk towards me on a floor of questions

Inconclusive aren't you, my love?

Your stable emotions shaken by intellect

You touch my soul with ambidextrous hands

And dive into my vision with quizzical eyes!

Your wants manifest,

Sometimes as can'ts and otherwise shouldn'ts

The purity of the heart and

The clarity of the mind gives

The surety of success, my love!

So anchor you thoughts

Wipe me off your sight

And make the decision,

I'll be beside, holding you

Either ways!
