When absurdities overwhelm the soul

And uncertainties continue to spike.

Realizing, intelligence is knowing

One's own depths of ignorance!

The question of deductions,

Being a boon or bane goes unanswered

Leaving bold question marks behind.

Ages of silence coupled with dark thoughts follow

Striping truth, unveiling the stark reality

Escaping the crude ice within is difficult

So use the warmth of the soul

And face the twirling icy winds!

Time doesn't heal anything, it just

Makes you get used to it.
