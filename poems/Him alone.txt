Wandering lonely over mountains and hills

In joy and happiness, your heart fills

With smiles and laughter you walk around

But you never know what is waiting

For your heart is always beating

There the darkness overwhelms light

All you need to do is put up a brave fight

Truths are hidden deep within, and

Hypnotized are you in the fantasy

Fatalists to embrace and death to kiss

But He is there with solitude and bliss

You hold His hand and walk along

Of praise and joy, you sing a song

Always a smile, never a spittle

He holds you high and makes you smile

For His grace is what you're showered upon

And never a delay, is a deny

Your years on earth are very few

For He and you are meant to be.
