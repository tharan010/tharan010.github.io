She entered the chamber with a steady gait

With eyes of innocence and

A heart of faith.

A sturdy woman, yet fragile,

Believing her broken pieces will be fixed.

But for him in the chamber,

Dressed in decency and eyes in disguised trust,

She was a prey!

With lustful eyes and a filthy self

He dug his nails into her flesh,

Weakening and molesting her.

A predator possessed by the evil demon

But the fragile flower,

She thought, she was being plucked to beautify the Gods

Little did she know she was the demon's prey

And just like every other handicapped battle

He exploited the victory!

Only to feel ashamed later

Beacause of a book long written,

Still yet to perceive the real meaning.
