Round and round I drift,

Like a lost astronaut in open space

Round and round.

I look down to see a tiny spot

Aimless without a backup.

I wonder if anyone can hear me screaming,

For I'm deafened in silence.

Can I please come down?

I'm tired of drifting round.

Confound, I'm talking to myself

Chanting long lost mythical lines.

In the emptiness of my mind

I now search for someone else.
