Dictated by crippled emotions,

We run errands on ourselves.

Drifting away in the mundane shamelessness,

We forget the important!

For as humane as I am,

I too shelter a demon within!

Blinded to the goodness of the world,

He stood there brave and still

None to hold, none to love

With soft eyes and a pleasant smile he strove

Never did he deter, an optimist he was;

The warmth of his heart spread love to the world!

But the ice in my heart made him shiver

The streaks of chills ran down his spine, and

As he froze he looked up, for help

All for me to flush him down

With the cruelty of my soul!

For then I realize,

It's not him that was blind!
