An unknown emotion dawns within,

Looking into her deep black eyes!

A feeling never felt before

Giving birth to butterflies in the stomach.

Her sight sending streams of impulses

Along every single nerve,

Shaking the cold, cruel and hardened body

An oblivious dragon is tamed with her touch!

A state of mind when sanity is dubious

And ecstasy to fill the emptiness.

The kiss of her altruistic love

Floods the brain with endorphins!

A blinding light races darkness,

To a mirage in surreal reality and

In hypothetical unawareness,

The greatest gamble ends

With the butterflies dying within.
