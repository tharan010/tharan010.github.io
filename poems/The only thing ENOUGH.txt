Bemused am I, in every aspect of life

With no one to severalize right and wrong 

Like a scroll in a corked bottle,

I float in the deep waters, lost.

The desires of the human mind is immense

Illogical in all senses, irrefutable is my word

For what taught me, is this world.

Tottering heads is what my eyes see

With a coseismic crown glistening through the filth of the mind

Ignorant is the mind to the morals of the heart

Wavering between right and wrong

The mind wanders, never to be found

Lost emotions and a false futuristic approach

Are the remnants of the bad decisions

Purposeful accidents are more frequent thereafter

Never is anything ever enough

The wisdom thus learned and irrefutable is that

Food is the only thing, ever ENOUGH for the human race

Hope for hope!
