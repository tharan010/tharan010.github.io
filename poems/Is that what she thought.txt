Standing on the edge,
There is no one around to judge
The joy of drowning into the night
No reason to put a fight.
Is that what she thought?

The heavy body, 
Now crushed into ice.
Merging with the elements of life
The beauty of oneness and peace!
Is that what she thought?

Being on fire did not hurt
.Broke out of the claustrophobic present
Into the vastness of the universe
The walls did not get closer anymore!
Is that what she thought?

Bottled up emotions now leak.
No one to stare or smirk
Free from the ugly reality
Anchored in the void of emptiness!
Is that what she thought?

The time is now, where the choice of Death overweighs life
Freed from life, in search of better!
Is that what she thought?
Or we?
