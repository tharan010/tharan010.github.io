A length of day begins torest,

Quiet and calm, the nightsettles.

A heart full of misery andbody sick,

The lost soul seeks solace inthe heavenly resemblance.

Hopeful eyes and the rampantmind begins to rest

But little did she know

About the venomous snakesneaking up to her!

Squirming along the lustfulmarsh

Sickening and wretched!

As her longing heart dreamedof cure

Rhythmic breathing like aprayer song

The fragrance of this obedientflower weakened

Only to be filled by thestench of the ugly snake!

As she lay, it crept up her

Devouring every inch of herbare skin!

Warmth and sweat from theunspeakable act,

Stained her shroud ofinnocence!

A nightmare she thought,

Fearfully opening her eyes

Oblivious to the precedingevents,

She continue to lay, the stenchpersisting

With no help or awareness to aid, 

She prays as she lay.

The ugly serpent slowlytransforming 

Takes the appearance ofbeautiful being,

With trustworthy and caring eyes,

The stench now, receding!

The hours of fear haunt hernow,

Forever a scar, deep andpainful

Every night a nightmare!

Yet, miserable and sick,With fearful andhopeful eyes

Thoughts too deep and weak,she lay

A heavenly resemblance theysaid,

But nothing about the lustfulsnake!
