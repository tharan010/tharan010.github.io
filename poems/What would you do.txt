What would you do, my soul?

Ever striving

To attain the unattainable

To convince the in convincible

To reach the unreachable

In this ever changing wheel of life.

When the music plays,

Would you sit in solace and

Immerge yourself in it? Or

Would you move your limbs

In accordance with it?

Would you sit in contemplation, or

Would you sing along?

What would you do, O soul?

When you walk on the wheel of life,

Would you go fast?

Would you slow slow down?

Or would you cling,

To your comfort zone and never revolve?

What would you do my soul?

What would you do!
