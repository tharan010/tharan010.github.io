There is a fire within, burning dark,

Roasting the unkept unknowns within

Ignited by sight and thought

She's a curse in disguise!

A fantasy she is, earning you the second ring

She continues to endeavor, tirelessly within

All her energy to take refuge in you

She sprouts every now, then

All to possess you!

But, you are a monkey!

You seek to find her, endlessly

Like a monkey jumping branches to reach the fruit

Just to realize its the cursed apple!

Blinded in lust you walk the streets of love

Never to know the difference between the two

But the day you do, you are old

And you regret, for

Time and wisdom, never go hand in hand.
