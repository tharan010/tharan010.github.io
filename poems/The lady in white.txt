Treading in loneliness

My thoughts are void.

Slow and steady my eye watches

Pictures and sights ignite flashes!

Then I realise it's the lady in white,

Lightning the night sky nice and bright,

Caught my eyes in that first sight

Making me think of her every night.

Mesmerized am I,

In her stunning looks

And glistened in her shiny eyes

These thoughts inside my head never die

For the fire in her eyes keeps me alive

Full stops never end my writing

Because I write about her

The lady in white.
