You define relativity!

Paradoxical aren't you?

Revealing who I really am, always

In the your fallible name!

Biased, from when

You knew yourself.

A transition you be

To the evil

But immovable you stand

For the good

Unpredictable are your complexities.

And now disoriented,

I see through muddy eyes

What I am and what I will be

As you, pass by!
