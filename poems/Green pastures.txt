Grazing the green pastures, he lay

Feet felt the hardness of the soil, but

The wounds of the heart were soothed.

Alone he walked, with an army's strength

Head high and barrel chested

He, had a gallantly stride!

Drifting beside quiet waters

He swam its depth,

In the shadow of death.

He knew no fear, but

Walked ahead, innocently brave.

And then in desolation he turned back

To see the great crowds

Of goodness and love to follow

He now, believes.
