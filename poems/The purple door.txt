I walk in the shadow of hidden walls

And there I find a purple door!

A glow of excitement beams through me

I open the purple door, a reflex!

To my wonder, time is slowed!

Precisely slowed, to enjoy and cherish

Every smile, thought, conversation and emotion!

A cloud of alien compassion sets in me,

All of it from the purple door!

An array of grace and elegance,

Truth and innocence I see!

With eyes of goodness and gold

She overdosed me with unknown emotions

To respect and love like never before!

I walk into the archway of hope

Strolling into the future with bright eyes

All through the purple door!

Then, the thought of slowed time lost

Drowning me into the misery I manifest.

Truths unfurl slowly, revealing reality

Knowing is knowing nothing at all!

A sadist am I in this world of stark surreality

Realizations dawn to hit hard, for

The slowed time is still slow

I suffer, inducing suffering

And now,

I plead forgiveness, to help not suffer

Its only you who can, and hence I plead

All through the purple door.
