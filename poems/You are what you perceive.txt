You are what you perceive, you are!

When you are angry,

You are the anger!

When you are jealous,

You are the jealously!

When you are lustful,

You are the lust!

You are what you perceive, you are!

When you are happy,

You are the happiness!

When you are loving,

You are the love,

When you are compassionate,

You are the compassion!

You are what you perceive, you are!

A flower lives its life being a flower,

Blooming with smiles!

A river lives its life being a river,

Ever flowing like thoughts!

A cloud lives its life being a cloud,

Shrouding over life!

If only we live being human beings,

We can welcome heaven on earth!

Hope for hope!
