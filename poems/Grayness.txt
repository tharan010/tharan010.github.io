This cruelty is dark

Like the night sky and the deep ocean

Swirling down into the depths of ambiguity

Leading to infinity, on and on

Multiple questions remain unanswered

The dream of life is now a lie

Like a nomad, I trot

In search of the grayness

No map in hand, or word to follow

I stray along this dark road,

Anticipating light,

But just when I see it,

Its nothing but the grayness!
