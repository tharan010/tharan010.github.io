Endure in worthiness, O my soul

To walk the carpet of humility.

Know, that the absence of

The feeling of self, is bliss.

Hopeless you may look

But endeavor humbleness,

In thought, in word and action.

For you were born innocent too.

Don't be a thorn, to prick

The hearts of innocent flesh

But be a flower, O my soul,

Give fragrance, to

The hand that crushes you.
