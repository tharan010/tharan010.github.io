On a spring summer morning

As the sweet Sun warms your soul,

You rest  in tranquility among the flowers!

Swirling into the lovely dreams of the day.

And as you are lost in the depths of euphoria,

You see a bee, swarming towards you

And he sits on your arm, slowly

Are you now intimidated?

That you shoo him away?

Is that fear in vulnerability?

To re-enter your place of solace?

But my dear, did you ever wonder?

The bee, mistook you for a flower!
