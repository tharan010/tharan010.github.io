Clouded with weirdness,

We settle in a confided space

To fill it with lost emotions.

Refractory to reality!

Blinded to the hard truth

We let madness takeover

Madmen are we,

Facing the stark reality.

Its not the metahuman power

But the dawning of cold truth

Harden ice within us both,

Warm the rest around

The call is yours my love,

For all I want, is to be with you

Until the universe does us apart.
