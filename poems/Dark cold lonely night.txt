As the city sleeps,

A loneliness sets in,

Like the snow flakes,

Falling slowly on a winter night!

The already dark sky, now darker

Sings a silent song.

The exhausted pen

Writes another hundreds words

Imprinting unspoken thoughts!

Breaths and words synchronizing

These words may never be read

But the fire burns, deep within

Never ceasing.

The thoughts of the dark cold lonely night,

Seek escape and refuge,

Only to find home in oneself.

You.
