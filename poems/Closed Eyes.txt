Its not the things that happened to you,

Made you who you are;

But the things you felt.

You are, not what you see you are,

You are more than that.

A universe resides in you!

Thou art magical!

When you kiss, you close your eyes!

When you dream, you close your eyes!

When you wish, you close your eyes!

When you pray, you close your eyes!

All to prove,

The most beautiful things in life are not seen, but felt!

Get to know the magical you, and fall in love.
