Dear lady, have you seen the tree?

The one outside the city?

The one that misses every eye?

The one with leaves of anger and pain!

The one with branches of vengeance and suffering!

The one with roots of ignorance!

It’s the tree feeding on misconceptions

Oblivious to the beauty around

And the continuous change,

The change with every breath!

Compassion, peace and life every day!

But my dear, the tree is long lost in ignorance

Denying the inevitable change

And dwelling in misconceptions and thoughts

O dear, such a tree needs help

And the peaceful community awaits,

To hug and heal.

But my dear woman, did you realize?

You are the lone tree.
