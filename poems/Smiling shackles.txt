A beautiful soul you are,

Born in this world as an angel!

Residing in shackles, you smile

Happy to serve the demons that haunt you.

Being chained to the loop of time,

Past, present and future

You continue to spread,

The warmth of your love!

So noble you are,

Educating millions and loving ruffians,

But helpless am I, seeing you suffer

A magic to formulate, I strive

To release you from the time loop

To give you the happiness you most deserve and

To love you like never before.
