You were a spark my friend

A spark that could enlighten the world

Your curious eyes could answer,

A million questions!

The innocence you gave birth to,

Every time you smiled!

The skill of you hand

Create life on paper!

Your shyness and compassion,

Made us love you deeper!

You loved and lived like never before

Smiles blooming around you!

I've always pictured you, grown fine,

Holding a scalpel, saving lives and

Making people happy, just not realizing,

You already did!

Every smile on your mother's face, you put

Every pride in your father's heart, you put

Every moment of happiness in your brothers, you put!

As the world mourns, my friend

I'd say you manifested deeply!

You may never breathe again

And I may never see you again

But you would be my brother,

A chosen one!
