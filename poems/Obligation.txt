Tears stream down, at the sight of the lines written,

Adds more meaning than the greatest of philosophies

The heart and mind syncs for this one time

To add meaning, to most of the things in life.

Questions, answered and tasks completed

An obligation, yet remains,

To fulfill the unfulfilled

In the best way unknown.

Like a chick looking at its mother,

My eyes glisten at the sight.

A mandatory obligation is set right up,

Blinding the pleasures and sorrow of the world

For the obligation is now, life.

Go steady my heart, listen to the soul,

Command the mind O soul, to abide by your rules,

Accomplice my body to regulate the planned.

To get the real out of me,

To add a greater meaning to life.
