How heavy is a speck of sand, my friend?

Does gravity spare its freedom?

What about a handful?

It is still, sadly bound,

By the force of this hardened earth!

Heavier it is!

Sinking into the depths, non-buoyant

Imagine the unison of particles sizing the moon

The power it holds is magnificent

Surging the blue waters at night.

The lightest to the heaviest in the world

Affects and is affected by the solid ground.

Cogitate now, brother!

Envisage, your thoughts have weight too

Just like the white sand on beaches

Gravity still clings on to it!

But thoughts,

They are bound to a liberal being,

And is free in space!

If the moon empowers tides, my friend

Think about the miracles your thoughts can do!

It can endue a million elements, in unity!

Be lucidly intellectual

What if two brothers are like minded?

What if a family thinks the same?

What if the whole of mankind,

Synchronized similar thoughts?

Unimaginable will be the strength

Overloading intangible and abstract power!

Wondrous and sorcerous it will be!

Unearthly, an alienated superpower!

So, is this my friend,

What the people in white, call PRAYER?
