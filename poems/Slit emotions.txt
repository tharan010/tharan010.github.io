Emotions and words with cut throats linger

And the tongue dying in a knot

I tread the battle ground

In mute dumbness!

Eyes give speeches which go unheard

The heart, a maker in virtual reality

All to express the unfelt emotion!

In disguise I feel your pleasures in me

And in the open I share your warmth

All to escape this tangled zone

From mixed relations,

I try to sprout. For,

In you I strive to flush my desires

In you I seek to find myself!
