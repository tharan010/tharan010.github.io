In the stones of silence we meet,

A place of solace to unite, the

Twin souls of varied origin.

Embedded in the cloud of emptiness

We whisper to respect the quiet.

The serene sound sweetens the air

And the ambience, lightens space.

When the warmth is shared,

We blush at our blooming roots

And smile at the perspiring flowers!

Eyes to sparkle and a mind of peace

We dwell in deep contemplation.

O dear, this feeling is sweet!

As our souls get closer,

The warmth is spread.

And at the first touch,

A fire is born;

The one that lightens the soul

Liberating every hidden emotion,

Unaltered affection flows into the ocean of the truth!

Holding hand in hand we sail,

To a mythical island of trust!
