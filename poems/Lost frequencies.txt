Disappearing into the insidious mystery

I'm found within lost frequencies!

Oblivious to the summer sun, 

I dwell in the darkness of the soul.

The easy steps to peace

And deep breaths to joy,

Just like you, I'm unaware.

Half smiles and deep breaths are the solution! 

I now refuse to believe?

Affected by the mundane reality

Of the obnoxious crowd around

I forgot to be the flower that I was once,

Sharing my beauty and fragrance, with pleasure.

A thorn am I now, 

Causing pain and seeing red.

Aimlessly I now trot.

In search of buried emotions, 

I now, walk the edge!
