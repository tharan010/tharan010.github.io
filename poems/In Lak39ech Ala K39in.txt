On this full moon I say to you, brother

You are my other me 

A spitting mirror image.

Of my deep deep, dark and crude self,

You stare into the whirlpool of my iris

Freezing ingenious thoughts of ages!

If I harm you,

I do harm to myself!

If I love you and respect you,

I love and respect myself!

So be it an hour from now,

A day, a fortnight or a moon from now,

Let's bind the unbound,

Cling to our flaws and embrace them in altruistic love!

For, you are my other me,

And I am your other you!
