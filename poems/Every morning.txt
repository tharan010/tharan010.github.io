An every morning blessing, that's what I get

A day of smiles and laughs to follow!

As always, me gazing into vacant space

Staring and figuring things out

Nothing has ever been right

For righteousness is in the heart and action.

Not in the appearance as I peruse it to be

For humble and simple is what I want to be!

Not the irresistible hard headedness with cruelty

Not the freezing ice from within.

An every morning blessing is what I get

That blessing is the most of all

My mother's warmth, my dad's affection,

And the love from my sister?

That is the blessing that I get, every morning.
