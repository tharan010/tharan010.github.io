An exploding white light

Is what she sees on a wave of ecstasy.

Every hair of her body to stand with my touch,

Unfelt and oblivious emotions are now born!

The air of uncertainty fills her lungs

A frightful excitement she feels,

To understand various parts of me

In mind, body and soul.

Hardened muscles carry her over

A cloud of senses, competing each other.

Lips touch in the sweetness of perspiration!

Every sense is now mindful

The hard and soft self completes each other

Filling every space, lost in eternity

She floats along her emotions, holding my hand

All to know and feel the unknown!

Now, a man is woman

And a woman is man!

Energies dissipate, lighting the universe

Everything, toying with love.
