Walking unnoticed, deliberately

Like my starboard Angel,

To inaugurate an unanticipated unification.

On a nauseous cloud I float,

Lost in direction and in thought

For I swamp in the long lost memories.

My heart beat races my fear,

My blood to RUSH nowhere.

I am rebel lost in peace.

Like an oxymoron I slog,

With heavy knees and droopy eyes

In hideousness, I hide.

An outbreak between the heart and mind begins

Feaful it is, a bloody cascade

The stimulant, a disastrous crusade

Blood drips from the devious dagger

My hands blooded, my soul stained

But my spirit endeavors, 

Thriving towards darkness.
