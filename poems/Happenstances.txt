Sinking into oblivion, being spiked

Incognizant to the stark reality,

Truth about lies, went unnoticed

The cold cruel heart is now frail.

The happenstances and words were

Just mere hallucinations, O soul

They continue burning bridges

And the unexplained emotions,

Leave behind stained memories.

Through them I see a shimmer

To realize time doesn't heal

But get you used to it and that

People don't change but become

More of who they really are.

You will feel a touch, my soul

Finer and warmer this time

It will hit you hard, but

Hold strong, for deeper roots take time

Hope for hope!
