The first be the virtuous pagans and the innocent.

Cries of infants echo in the caves of Limbo,

Swamped in desperation, they seek the Holy!

Second be the souls whose lust is blown,

Violently they spiral into thunderstorms!

Because the glutton is cold inside

He's third to suffer in freezing slush

And in vile he lives.

In smelting gold, the avaricious rot

Being fourth in the knot

They claw their way out, to be pulled back.

In war are the angered,

Swamped in immovable marsh

Fifth are they in the surface of Styx

In the burning pit are the heterodoxy,

And demons whip the fleeing souls.

These suffer the sixth.

In a lake if boiling blood, drown the violent!

The blasphemous are burnt

As the seventh sacrifice

The seductive panderers are hammered in darkness

Those crowned for fraud, are the eighth!

Trophy is for the treacherous!

Betrayers are statued in ice waist below

Immovable are they in a freezing lake

Satan is centered, with his vicious whispering

Waswasa! waswasa! waswasa!
