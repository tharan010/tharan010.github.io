You are excellence; the incomparable one, my child

The world you strive in, is fairyland no more!

Do not feel heavy of the ones who hurt you,

Spread that pretty smile like you always do,

For the ones who hurt you, are hurt themselves!

The ones who make you cry are crying within!

Like them or hate them;

Stick to no notions my girl

Compassion is key,

Walk with ignorance never!

Be the better of the rest!

See the world with sparkling eyes

All to realize,

You are the good in everything good,

You are the anger in every frown,

And also the happiness in every smile!

Brighten this world , my child

With the light of your eyes!

Let your kohl beautify its ugliness

Hiding the horror in the depths of the darkness

And let the scent of your hair, refresh the air we breathe!

For you are the good in everything good,

You are the anger in every frown

And also the happiness in every smile!
