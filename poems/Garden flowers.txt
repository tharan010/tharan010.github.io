Walking beside still waters

The cold morning breeze graces me

To invoke thoughts of infinite strength!

Flowers are we in each others garden

Swaying with sweet fragrance

Enriching the air around

Adding beauty to each other

O how beautiful we are!

As tender as our souls

In consciousness we unite!

The enlightened one said,

To like is to pluck a flower but

To love is to water it everyday.

The truth nails desires

For all I want is to water you,

But be plucked.
