I was standing there, ahead of all

In a shiny suit, three men to my side

In the tense air, I wait

To see a child, flower your way.

There I see you, in Angel eyes

Walk the aisle holding a strong arm

With long flowing hair, you were in white!

You reach close, and let go to tremble

You hold my arm,

There my knees buckle and

Vision unstable, angled to you.

No speeches or orations made

Just words spoken,

"I do, I do"

And then my lips were on yours!

That second I feared nothing,

Isn't that happiness?

This was all a dream,

But without you,

It never came true!
