There is a search, the one within

Like the blind in the gallows

Searching for the one

That cannot be found.

Hope, to fuel this heart

And to fool this mind

I stroll ahead, without a sway.

The road ahead looks long

The distance meets the eye.

Like parallel lines they stretch

Meeting at infinity.

A speck am I in this long journey

To the destination, unknown.

My map, is the Word

Never known.
