What you and me don't deserve

But always enjoy with pleasure.

Realizations lost long within,

Memories lost and the tribute unpaid,

The melodious ears turned deaf,

The desired eye turned blind,

The pleasure seeking mind turned mad

But the love seeking heart

Always loved

For its Grace that saved you

Open the blindfold to His majestic appearance

Hear to the words and praise of Our God

Shout and sing in joy, for ever more

Praise Him and all

For Grace is our call!
