A blurred visual, of anonymity to begin

Ages of sopor, to follow.

The dark big eyes, now seek the inglorious.

Every word; heard, spoken and thought

Are inked into the skin of the mind.

Convolutions to prove linear growth

Intelligence to unlock the mysteries of the mind

And love, that of the soul.

Deep breaths to stabilize the rebellious mind and body

The rivals have now, grown up together

The two swordsmen stare at the other

To witness, immense energy!

You are your most valuable possession!

Protect and provide!
