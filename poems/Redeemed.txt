The black revolution begins

After the ages of cremation.

From ashes, it springs to life

Filled with hatred and vengeance.

Behold, the cold cruelty is unleashed in the end.

Is this doomed darkness destined to me?

Or am I craving to sink into the depths of darkness?

Truth be told, life is lost when the purpose is known!

Stuttering and mumbling in darkness,  days go on

The world of false hopes, shattered after the truth.

The greatest question is yet masked

Death, is the only answer

For thats when wisdom is known.

Life, is a game, of mortal combat

Survival of the most able.

Then I see a bright light

This one, with origin unknown

Brightening the darkness of this world

The One that shadows light

The One that redeemed.
