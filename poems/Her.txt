Her pattering little feet,

Conquering every inch on the floor

Voice so soft and sweet

Can never let my heart be sore.

Running around her palace

Like a princess in solace

Fills the air around,

Whispering to the wind.

Whipped cream to fill her dream,

And a scare to wake her up

Her greatest trouble, choosing colors

For a painting she is.

Her tender little heart will mold,

The prettiest smile she wears.

The child in her will never go

But just like me, she will too

One day grow.
