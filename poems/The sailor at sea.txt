The echoing sound of rhythmicity I hear

And to count the innumerable, I try

But fail like other parts of my life

I awaken at the smell of it

And let my mind wander

Staring into the nebulousness,

The darkness enhances my sight.

To see the silhouettes of heavens

I strain my eyes, to see clearer

Like a sailor with his telescope

But unlike him,

I'm lost at sea without a compass

Destination being long lost white sands

With purse lips and constrained eyes

I look at the sea

And all it holds for me.
