He said to his brother, 

My belongings are yours

Treasure them like you, and

Be the man you've always been.

He said to his sister, 

You never fought the battle

Because your opponent was me, now

Stride in pride, victorious; for

The world awaits a war with you.

He said to his father, 

My love was abeyant

Just like yours

And if a gallant I am 

To you, I salute.

He said to his mother, 

Your love can separate us never.

I will come, 

After hoisting the flag

Or wrapped in it.

Then he said to his love, 

Forget me not, nor your love for me 

For I am a soldier, born to die.

The things he said, he meant

But now, he has fallen.
