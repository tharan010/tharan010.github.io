The absurdities continue to exist

In the vagueness of an empty night,

The soul attains satiety.

And the brains talk in synchrony,

A flood of memories.

Infantile or grown up

The difference seems none!

A lawn to steal our footsteps

A night to hold our thoughts

And an eternity to feel,

What was never felt before!

To move along the waves of love

And not make a bond with land, is unfeasible

For the ocean and land,

Always meet.
