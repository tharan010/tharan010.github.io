Fighting between the black and white

I dwell in the ambiguity of greyness

With rivers to cross and mountains to climb

A long journey lies ahead

But here I sit on this Hilltop

Sinking myself in time

The questions arise

Of right and wrong but

I tread in the greyness

Only to pretend to be strong

Where the journey leads?

I know not, the answer!

But I continue to walk, in the greyness to transfer

When the lands are marshy and soil loose

When you know it will collapse

Will you still build your empire?

Lost again, in the depths of indecisive inconclusiveness

Only to hope for hope!
