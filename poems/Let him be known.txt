Born and known, strong and sturdy!

He spent his days in the evil shelter

No strap, no chains,

Nothing could hold him down.

He was a Knight, a wild hound!

And lived by principles of his own and no others!

With debatable honor he strayed

Witnessing death many a times,

He fled loose again and again.

For there was a force within him

Driving him, his only purpose!

And when the day came,

He fought bravely,

Against his own failures

A battle to be lost in vain!

His strong cold heart wouldn’t let go,

An eye for an eye,

He daringly devoured his enemy!

He lay among the ashes

With victory embedded on this scarred body!

The man of integrity,

Had also loved, to his capability

And now, forever will he be remembered.
