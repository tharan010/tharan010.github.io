Memories flood the newly filled spaces of emptiness within

Every time this downpour happens, the soul takes away,

Strolling in the same piece of white sand

Lost in time I stand.

I see the things of the past, with closed eyes

As peaceful as they used to be, memories.

An ostentatious display, an exotic splurge

Endorphins fill my brain llike it always did.

The whole theory is just chemistry, I realize

A gameplay of chemicals in a confined sanctuary.

The one sheltering my chemical laboratory

All this until I met Liberty,

To whom I then seeked refuge

All because a mad scientist lost his lab

The endorphins swamped me again

This time without bonds,

I could fly!

And then these images flash again

Sowing fear within the scared soul.

Until you held hands, to kill my fear

But to provoke my anger

With this emotion to die, I wait for sunrise

For sunrise and Him

That is peace.
