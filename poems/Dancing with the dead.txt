Treading along the country road,

Embracing the wind in my hair

In the emptiness of my mind, I'm lost.

I walk into the dark wilderness

Intimidated by the dark silence,

I continue, long enough to hear

The trampling of autumn leaves filling the air

To my expected fear I see them

Sturdy, strong and dark, they appear

Phoenix heads tattooed, on every barrel chest

This sight kindles an emotion, never felt before

In the eyes I see, captivating spirits

Spiraling down to the depths of their souls!

They lead me to their darkest den

There I see a fire,

Burning brighter than the one in their eyes

Making me look like a liar.

They led me to its center,

To witness the most ferocious dance ever

The sight that I captured, I'd never forget

Bewitched to this mindless dance, I too move

Dancing with the dead.
