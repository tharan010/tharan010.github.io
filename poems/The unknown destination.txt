Deafening silence, is what you pursue

O soul, for in solitariness you redeem

The wisdom, to unlock the mysteries of your world

Dubious to existence, you wander the lone city

In crowned curiosity, you march

To be light, in dead darkness.

Your presence is vulnerable

For that is love, you say.

Immaculate you are,

Impeccable is your love, like mother's.

My thoughts continue to make way

On a deserted land

To a destination that

Always remained, unknown.
